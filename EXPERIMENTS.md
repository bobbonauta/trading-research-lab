This is the single canonical experiment register for Trading Research Lab. No other experiments or strategy variants are recorded.

## EXP-001 — Daily long-only SMA crossover

- **State:** NON VERIFICATA; the source review identified a conditional provider candidate, but no authenticated access or market dataset was verified. The economic backtest remains blocked; this is not an economic rejection of the hypothesis.
- **Hypothesis:** price trends may persist long enough to produce a statistical advantage after realistic costs; any effect may be absent or regime-dependent.
- **Proposed rule:** on daily bars, enter long when SMA(20) crosses above SMA(100); exit when SMA(20) crosses below SMA(100). Signals use closed bars; orders are attempted at the next bar open. No short positions, leverage, or concurrent positions.
- **Sizing and risk mechanics in the prototype:** buy whole units using available cash only; one position at a time; no stop is used, so loss can reach the invested amount. Any future use must confirm this risk rule for the selected instrument.
- **Gap and order assumptions in the prototype:** next-bar open reference price, with modeled spread and slippage; assume full fills. Insufficient cash for one unit is recorded as a rejected order. Missing/inconsistent OHLC data stops the run. Any open position is liquidated at the final bar close.
- **Instrument:** SPY (US-listed ETF), daily bars, is the proposed candidate only; it is not frozen pending data access and owner approval.
- **Data and provenance:** Alpaca Market Data API is a conditional source candidate reviewed on 2026-11-05. No authenticated request or dataset was obtained, so actual SPY coverage, quality, and access under the user account remain unverified. No market data was copied or published.
- **Development/validation/test periods:** proposed calendar windows are 2016–2021 / 2022–2023 / 2024–2025. Actual rows, gaps, and trade counts have not been checked; adequacy is not established by calendar duration alone.
- **Costs and benchmark:** a provisional model is proposed below, but no broker schedule or historical spread evidence was verified; values are not frozen. Benchmark: SPY buy-and-hold with matching capital, costs, and distribution treatment.
- **Engine and code version:** `backtest.py`, SHA-256 `8570FE426700E16809802414F489DD424AA6BF87AF4035D790F3C48A237F6A97`; no external engine or package installation. The engine is a prototype and has no benchmark or market-data ingestion layer.
- **Technical checks:** `python -m unittest -v test_backtest` passed 8/8 synthetic tests on Python 3.11.5. Checks cover next-bar entry/exit timing, cost and P&L reconciliation, losing trades, no-trade periods, insufficient cash, missing/inconsistent data, and reproducibility. The first run failed because `fmean` was imported from `math`; the import was corrected and the full suite then passed.
- **Attempts/variants:** zero market-data backtests; one proposed rule; no tuning or parameter variants.
- **Results:** NOT RUN on market data. No P&L, performance, risk, benchmark, or trade metrics are available for a real instrument.
- **Decision:** STOP before any economic backtest. Do not treat provider documentation as verification of a downloaded dataset or synthetic checks as market results. Required motor gaps and remaining gates are recorded below.
- **Next required evidence:** authorized authenticated data access; a local immutable dataset snapshot with verified rights, coverage and quality; approval of the proposed instrument, capital, costs and benchmark; adequate chronological samples; and minimal dividend/split and benchmark support in the existing engine.

### TRL-002 data-gate review and proposed specification (2026-11-05)

**Source review.** Alpaca documents US stock/ETF historical bars from 2016 and a SIP feed sourced from CTA/UTP; its free Basic plan requires historical SIP requests to end at least 15 minutes before the current time. The API requires authentication, and no account/key access was verified or used. The candidate request is `SPY`, `timeframe=1Day`, `feed=sip`, `adjustment=raw`, from `2016-01-01` through `2025-12-31`. IEX is rejected for this test because it is a single venue. Provider documentation describes split/dividend adjustments and a corporate-actions endpoint, but also disclaims accuracy/completeness guarantees and says corporate-action records may be delayed. References: [market-data plans](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/about-market-data-api), [historical feed FAQ](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/market-data-faq), [bar adjustments](https://alpaca.markets/learn/fetch-historical-data), [corporate actions](https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/reference/corporateactions-1).

Alpaca's posted terms limit use to personal, non-commercial purposes and prohibit public posting or redistribution of its content without prior written consent. Therefore no raw dataset may enter this public repository; public release of derived market-data results also needs a separate terms review. The proposed source is not currently accessible in this workspace without authenticated credentials, which this order did not authorize creating or connecting. References: [Alpaca terms](https://files.alpaca.markets/disclosures/library/TermsAndConditions.pdf), [SPY issuer page and historical distributions](https://www.ssga.com/us/en/individual/etfs/state-street-spdr-sp-500-etf-trust-spy).

**Data period and adequacy.** If access is later authorized, use the fixed full-year window `2016-01-01`–`2025-12-31`: development `2016-01-01`–`2021-12-31`, validation `2022-01-01`–`2023-12-31`, final test `2024-01-01`–`2025-12-31`. These are proposed boundaries, not a claim that a 60/20/20 calendar split is statistically adequate. Before opening the final test, verify actual bars by session/year, duplicates and gaps against the US market calendar, OHLC consistency, source timestamps/session dates, and completed-trade counts in each window. The first 100 daily bars are warm-up only. Keep one continuous strategy/equity path across boundaries; do not reset positions or tune on the final segment. If test trades are too sparse for the frozen inference criteria, classify the result as inconclusive rather than expanding or reshaping the test after seeing returns. No numeric minimum trade count or economic promotion threshold has yet been approved, so the final test remains locked.

**Proposed frozen mechanics, pending approval.** Keep SMA20/SMA100 unchanged, long-only, signal at completed close, fill at next session open, one position, whole shares, cash-only, no leverage or stop, and liquidate at the final close. Proposed initial capital is USD 10,000. The risk cap is the full available capital; loss may approach the entire invested amount. Use raw OHLC for signals and execution. Apply split events to share quantity on their effective dates; accrue a cash-dividend receivable when entitled on the ex-date, convert it to cash on the payable date, and do not reinvest strategy dividends. Cross-check SPY distribution records against the issuer history. Map each provider daily timestamp to its US market session date and retain the raw timestamp/timezone in the manifest.

Provisional deterministic cost inputs for review: commission `1 bp` per fill; full quoted spread `2 bps` (the engine charges half on each side); slippage `1 bp` per side. Stress case doubles each input. These are transparent model assumptions, not observed broker fees or measured SPY spreads; actual costs remain a blocker until accepted or replaced by verified execution-cost evidence. Benchmark proposal: invest the same USD 10,000 in SPY at the first session open, buy-and-hold through the same dates, apply the same entry/exit costs and split/dividend accounting, and reinvest paid distributions at the next session open using whole shares and the same costs.

Primary reported outputs: net total return and excess return versus that benchmark for each chronological window; daily-equity maximum drawdown; trade count, win rate, profit factor, expectancy, exposure and total costs; repeat with doubled costs. Sharpe is omitted unless the daily return series and annualization convention are frozen and the sample is adequate. This order does not promote EXP-001 to candidate or authorize paper trading.

**Minimum changes needed before a market run.** Reuse `backtest.py`; do not add another engine. Add a small importer/manifest for the selected vendor response, extend the same accounting path to handle split quantities and dividend receivables, add the matched buy-and-hold benchmark and window metrics, then test these mechanics synthetically and reconcile cash/equity. No code or data changes were made in this review. The engine currently has no data-ingestion layer, corporate-action/dividend accounting, or benchmark. Its fixed bps model assumes full fills, does not measure spread from quotes, and liquidates at final close. The existing eight synthetic tests do not cover dividends, splits, or benchmark reconciliation.
